Our Story

Started with a grill. Built into a neighborhood spot.

Kings Grill was created in 2016 by fictional founders Marcus King and Andre Ellis, two Philadelphia friends who believed good food didn't need to be complicated.

Their idea was simple: bring people together around a hot grill, serve generous portions and make every plate taste like somebody actually cared about it.

What started as a small neighborhood concept eventually became a six-day-a-week grill house known for burgers, steaks, wings and the smell of charcoal drifting down the block.

"Good food brings people to the table. Great food keeps them there."
