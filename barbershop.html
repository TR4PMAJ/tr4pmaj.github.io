Our Story

Built on Broad Street. Kept alive by Philly.

Liberty Line started in 2014 when two longtime friends, Marcus Bell and Andre Williams, decided Philadelphia needed another barbershop where people could actually feel at home.

They rented a small storefront just off North Broad with three chairs, two clippers and a hand-painted sign. Word traveled quickly.

Today, Liberty Line has grown into a six-chair shop without losing the neighborhood feel that got people through the door in the first place.

"A good haircut gets you ready for the week. A good barber remembers your name."
— Marcus Bell, Co-Founder
